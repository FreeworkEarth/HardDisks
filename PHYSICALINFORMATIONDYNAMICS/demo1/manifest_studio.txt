## what is compared
Per pair of runs: stdout byte for byte (cmp), with no line excluded (both sides use one compiler and one flag set, so
even the build line is equal), and stderr must be empty. The harnesses print event hashes, event counts, health and
audit counters, contact-audit maxima, KE drift and Z, but no final positions. box_final_state prints every disk's final
position, velocity and collision count, the bodies, the impulse, virial and tie accessors, the collision-hook digest,
the event-log digest, a checkpoint-restart verdict, and the health, audit, ledger and tolerance records (in %a or raw
bytes). Each output also passes its own verdicts (exact cell, row and line counts; every verdict line; the gen3 health
counters 0; the last line) so that a failure shared by both sides is not read as agreement.
Tests 4-6 and 10 (prim_tests, prim_controls) and 7 (pid_demo1) judge their own runs and print one "check" line per criterion;
the outputs are kept here in full. Test 8 compares the data files of runs A-D of test 7 byte for byte; test 9 sums the
density files of B against final.txt independently (awk).
Cloud (Linux) and Mac hashes differ for the same sources (pre-check A7): compare only within one machine and compiler.

## what is compared
Per pair of runs: stdout byte for byte (cmp), with no line excluded (both sides use one compiler and one flag set, so
even the build line is equal), and stderr must be empty. The harnesses print event hashes, event counts, health and
audit counters, contact-audit maxima, KE drift and Z, but no final positions. box_final_state prints every disk's final
position, velocity and collision count, the bodies, the impulse, virial and tie accessors, the collision-hook digest,
the event-log digest, a checkpoint-restart verdict, and the health, audit, ledger and tolerance records (in %a or raw
bytes). Each output also passes its own verdicts (exact cell, row and line counts; every verdict line; the gen3 health
counters 0; the last line) so that a failure shared by both sides is not read as agreement.
Tests 4-6 and 10 (prim_tests, prim_controls) and 7 (pid_demo1) judge their own runs and print one "check" line per criterion;
the outputs are kept here in full. Test 8 compares the data files of runs A-D of test 7 byte for byte; test 9 sums the
density files of B against final.txt independently (awk).
Cloud (Linux) and Mac hashes differ for the same sources (pre-check A7): compare only within one machine and compiler.
