How to Get Koa Access for an External Collaborator

If you are an active UH faculty or staff member, you can sponsor an external research collaborator to give them access to the Koa cluster.

The process involves two main stages: first, getting them official UH credentials, and second, having them apply for a Koa account.

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Sponsor a UH Affiliate Account for Your Collaborator

Before your collaborator can access Koa, they must have an official UH username and password. You, as the sponsor, must request a UH Affiliate Account on their behalf.

  • Action: Follow the official ITS process to request an affiliate account for your collaborator.

  • Details can be found here: UH Affiliate Account Information

Step 2: Collaborator Applies for a Koa Account

Once their UH Affiliate Account is active and they have their UH credentials, your collaborator can apply for a Koa account just like any other member of the UH community.

  • Action: Direct your collaborator to the standard Koa account request and onboarding process.

After their application is approved, they will have access to Koa's resources for the duration of their affiliate status.
