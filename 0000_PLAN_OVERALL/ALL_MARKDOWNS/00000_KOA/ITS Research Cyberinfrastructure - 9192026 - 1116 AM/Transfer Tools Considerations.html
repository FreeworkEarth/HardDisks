Using Data Transfer Tools with MFA/DUO

When you transfer files to Koa, how you handle the DUO (MFA) prompt depends on the tool you are using.

Method 1: SSH-Based Tools (scp, rsync, FileZilla)

These common tools use the SSH protocol, which requires a DUO two-factor authentication for every new connection. This can cause issues, especially with graphical programs.

The Challenge: Multiple Connections

Programs like FileZilla often try to open multiple connections at once to speed up transfers. This will either fail or bombard you with multiple DUO push notifications.

Solution for FileZilla:

To ensure a smooth connection, you must change two settings in FileZilla's Site Manager for your Koa connection:

  1. In the General tab, set the Logon Type to Interactive.

  2. In the Transfer Settings tab, check the box for Limit number of simultaneous connections and set the Maximum number of connections to 1.

This forces FileZilla to use a single connection that can correctly show you the DUO prompt.

Tip for scp and rsync: If your command-line transfer seems to hang, check your primary DUO device. It may have received a "silent push" that is waiting for your approval.

Method 2: Globus

Globus is the easiest and most reliable option for frequent or large data transfers, as it avoids these MFA issues entirely.

  • How it Works: Globus uses its own web-based authentication. You log in and approve DUO once through your browser.

  • The Benefit: After logging in, you can start as many transfers as you need without any further DUO prompts. Globus manages the transfer in the background, even if you close your computer.
