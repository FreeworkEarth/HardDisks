CPU Nodes

As Koa is the 3rd generation central HPC cluster at UH, in which it has consumed previous cluster generation hardware, it currently has a long tail of different types of CPUs available to users.  The CPUs available to users go as far back as Circa 2012 with the Intel Ivy-bridge architecture and reach into modern day Intel with the Xeon 6 series,  Granite Rapids, and  AMD's EPYC 4th generation Genoa .
