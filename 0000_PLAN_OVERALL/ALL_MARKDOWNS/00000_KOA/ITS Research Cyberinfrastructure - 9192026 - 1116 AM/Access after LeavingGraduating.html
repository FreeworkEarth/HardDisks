What Happens to Your Koa Account When You Leave UH?

Access to the Koa high-performance computing cluster is a resource for actively affiliated students, faculty, and staff of the University of Hawaiʻi. When your official affiliation with UH ends (e.g., upon graduation or departure), your access to Koa will be removed by default.

Please Note: Becoming part of the UH ʻOhana (alumni network) does not count as an active affiliation for the purpose of retaining Koa access.

How to Request an Access Extension

We understand that you may need to continue using Koa to finalize publications or collaborations after you depart. To support this, you can request a sponsored extension of your access.

Requirements for an Extension:

To be eligible for an access extension, you must meet the following criteria:

  1. Find a Sponsor: You must have an active UH faculty or staff member who agrees to sponsor your continued access.

  2. Maintain Your UH Credentials: You must opt-in to the UH ʻOhana program upon departure to keep your UH email address and credentials active.

  3. Renew Annually: The extension is granted in one-year increments and must be renewed by your sponsor each year.

For the Sponsor: How to Submit a Request

The sponsoring faculty or staff member must send an email to the HPC help desk containing the following information:

  • The full UH email address of the person they are sponsoring.

  • A brief description of why the person requires an access extension to Koa (e.g., "to complete work on our joint publication, Project XYZ").
