KoaCloud: Cloud Storage for Researchers

KoaCloud is a subscription storage service that works like Dropbox or Google Drive.

⚠️ Important: KoaCloud is a separate service and does not connect to the Koa high-performance computing cluster. Use it for storing and sharing files from your personal and lab computers.

Who Should Use KoaCloud?

This service is ideal for UH researchers who need to:

  • Store and sync files across multiple devices (laptops, lab computers, etc.).

  • Share data easily with internal and external collaborators.

  • Have a cloud-based storage solution separate from the Koa cluster.

Subscription Details

  • 💰 Cost: $55 per year for each 1 TiB of storage.

  • Storage Size: Purchased in 1 TiB increments.

  • File Limit: ✅ No limit on the number of files or folders.

  • How to Access: Access your files through a web browser or desktop/mobile apps (powered by Nextcloud).
