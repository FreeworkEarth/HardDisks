Koa's Idle Account Policy

To maintain a secure environment, Koa user accounts are automatically locked after a period of inactivity. This policy helps protect your data and the cluster from potential security risks associated with dormant accounts.

How the Policy Works: A Timeline

  • After 90 Days of Inactivity: You will receive an automated email warning you that your account is scheduled to be locked in 30 days.

  • After 120 Days of Inactivity: Your account is automatically locked, and you will be unable to log in.

What Happens if My Account is Locked?

  • Your Data is Safe: All of your files and data in your home directory and other permanent storage areas are not deleted. They remain untouched.

  • You Cannot Log In: Your ability to access the system is temporarily suspended.

  • How to Restore Access: To unlock your account, simply email the HPC help desk to request that it be reactivated.

How to Keep Your Account Active

To prevent your account from being locked, you only need to log in to Koa at least once every 120 days.
