How to Find Answers and Get Help

When you encounter an issue on Koa, following these steps can help you find a solution quickly and efficiently.

Step 1: Check the Official Koa Documentation First

Our online documentation is the best place to start. It covers the most common questions, policies, and procedures for using the Koa cluster. Many issues can be resolved by consulting these resources.

  • Resource Documentation & User Guide: For technical guides and how-tos.

  • Information & Policies: For cluster rules and information.

  • Video Tutorials: For visual walkthroughs of common tasks.

Step 2: Search Online for Specific Software Errors

If your problem is related to a specific application (e.g., TensorFlow, R, Python libraries) and not the Koa system itself, a general web search is often the fastest way to find a solution.

  • Tip: Copy the exact error message and paste it into a search engine like Google.

  • Check the software's official forums or GitHub Issues page. It is very likely that another researcher has encountered the same problem.

Step 3: Contact the Help Desk (If All Else Fails)

If you've checked the documentation and searched online without success, our team is here to help.  Please contact the HPC help desk.

The Best Way to Reach Us

For the fastest response, please email us directly at our dedicated help desk address:

  • UH-HPC-Help@lists.hawaii.edu

Using this email is the most direct way to reach the Koa support staff. Contacting the general ITS help desk will result in a delay, as your ticket will need to be forwarded to our team.

What to Include in Your Email for Faster Support

Providing the right information upfront helps us diagnose and solve your issue much more quickly. Please find the category below that best matches your problem and include the requested details in your email.

1. For Issues with a Submitted Job (Batch or Web Portal)

If your problem is related to a specific job, please include:

  • Job ID: (e.g., 1234567)

  • Session ID: (For web portal jobs, found in "My Interactive Sessions")

  • Working Directory: The full path to your job's folder.

  • Error/Output Files: The names and location of your job's .err and .out files.

  • A brief description of what went wrong.

2. For General Issues (Software, Connection Problems, etc.)

If you are encountering an error that is not tied to a specific job, please provide:

  • A clear description of the problem you are facing.

  • The exact steps you took that led to the error.

  • A copy-and-pasted, verbatim copy of the full error message.

3. For Login Issues

If you are unable to log in to Koa, the most common reasons are a change in your UH status or an account locked due to inactivity (over 120 days).

  • Please state in your email that you are having trouble logging in and ask us to check if your account has been locked.
