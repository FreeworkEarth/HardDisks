What are Partitions?

A partition is a group of compute nodes with a specific set of rules. Think of them as different queues at an airport—some are for priority boarding, and others are for general passengers. Choosing the right partition for your job is essential for getting your work done efficiently.

Each partition on Koa has its own rules, such as:

  • Maximum job runtime (time limit)

  • Which users can access it

  • The type of hardware available (e.g., CPUs only, specific GPUs)
