Modules

The central repository of software on Koa is not managed through the typical package managers one would come to expect when working with Linux.  As a result, all software is typically built from source, including many of the dependencies that a software may call for.  To also aid in reproducibility, the central repository of software on Koa and on other HPC systems may have multiple version of the same software.  As result, a method to dynamically affect a users session environment is needed.

On many HPC systems, including Koa, some type of software manager is installed, with a common name of modules.  In the case of Koa, we utilize what is known as Lmod to act as our software module manager.
