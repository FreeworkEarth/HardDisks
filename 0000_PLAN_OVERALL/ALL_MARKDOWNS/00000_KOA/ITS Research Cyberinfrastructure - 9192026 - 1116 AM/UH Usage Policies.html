UH Policies

The infrastructure provided by ITS Research Cyberinfrastructure, know collectively as Koa, is governed by Hawaii State law and the University of Hawaii Executive Policies.  Please be aware of that we will never ask for or require your password.  Please also read and understand what can and cannot be done while using Koa.

Security Regulations

While all attempts are made to make sure Koa is secure and follows best practices when possible, Koa is only designated to process and hold data that can be classified as Public (No Risk) or Restricted (Low Risk) data based on the UH data classification standards: https://www.hawaii.edu/infosec/minimum-standards.

While not exhaustive, examples of the type of data that is not acceptable to be stored or processed on Koa include:

  • Classified Data
  • Clinical Data (HIPAA/PHI)
  • International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) data
  • Export Administration Regulations (EAR) data
  • Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) data
  • Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)
  • Sensitive But Unclassified (SBU)
  • Data with similar data classifications
