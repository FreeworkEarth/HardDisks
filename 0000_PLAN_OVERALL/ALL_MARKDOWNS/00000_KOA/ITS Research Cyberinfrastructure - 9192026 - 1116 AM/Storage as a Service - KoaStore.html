KoaStore: Paid, High-Performance Storage

KoaStore is a subscription service for labs and users who need more permanent storage space than the free options provide.

Subscription Details

  • 💰 Cost: $50 per year for each 1 TiB block of storage.

    What You Get (per 1 TiB block):

    • Storage Space: 1 TiB

    • File/Folder Limit: 1,048,576

  • How to Pay: Payment is accepted through RCUH and UH accounts only.

Key Features

  • High Performance: Ideal for jobs that need fast data access, without the time limits of koa_scratch.

  • Customizable: Purchase as many 1 TiB increments as your lab needs.

  • Permanent: Your files are not automatically deleted.

✅ How to Manage Your KoaStore

  1. Check Your Quotas Regularly Use the usage command to monitor your storage space and file count. A job can fail if it hits a limit.

  2. Organize Your Data Since this space may be shared, create a clear folder structure (e.g., separate folders for each user and for shared datasets).

  3. Handle Small Files Efficiently For best performance, if you have many small, read-only files, group them into a single squashfs archive.

  4. File Size Recommendation: KoaStore is optimized for an average file size of at least 1 MiB. For many small, read-only files, consider using a squashfs archive to save space and improve performance.

⚠️ Critical: You Are Responsible for Backups

Data on KoaStore is NOT backed up by the system. You must back up your own important files to a separate, external location to prevent permanent data loss.
