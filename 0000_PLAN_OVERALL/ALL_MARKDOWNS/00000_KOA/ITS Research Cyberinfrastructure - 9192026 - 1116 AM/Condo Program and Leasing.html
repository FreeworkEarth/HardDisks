The Condo Leasing Program:
Renting Computing Power on Koa

The leasing program allows University of Hawaii (UH) faculty and staff to rent dedicated computational resources (a "condo") on the Koa cluster for a specific period.

Think of it as renting an apartment instead of buying one. You get exclusive, priority access to a set of resources for your project or lab, without the long-term commitment of a hardware purchase.

Key Benefits

  • Flexible Terms: Lease for as short as one month or as long as one year.

  • Priority Access: Your jobs get priority on the resources you lease.

  • Extended Runtimes: Jobs running on your leased condo can run for up to 30 days. 

Callout

Leasing is a service subscription, not a capital equipment purchase.  Depending on the funding sources, this may have an impact on indirect costs.
