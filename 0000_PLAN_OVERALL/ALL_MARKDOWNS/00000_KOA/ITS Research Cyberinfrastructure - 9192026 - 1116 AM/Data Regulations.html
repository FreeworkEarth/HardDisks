What type of data can be stored on Koa

Koa is designed to handle Public (No Risk) or Restricted (Low Risk) data based on the UH data classification standards: https://www.hawaii.edu/infosec/minimum-standards.

What to AVOID Storing

Do NOT store the following types of data on Koa:

  • Classified Data
  • Clinical Data (HIPAA/PHI)
  • International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) data
  • Export Administration Regulations (EAR) data
  • Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) data
  • Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)
  • Sensitive But Unclassified (SBU)
  • Data with similar data classifications

Need Help?

If you're unsure whether your data is safe to store on Koa, please reach out to us at uh-hpc-help before uploading any data.
