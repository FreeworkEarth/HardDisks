Storage Limitations

While the storage systems used on Koa maintain a level of redundancy in the case of hardware failure across multiple levels, this does not constitute a backup.

On Koa, all user storage is not backed up. This means you are responsible for your own data.

What this means for you

  • No Backups: Koa's storage systems have redundancy to protect against hardware failure, but this is not a backup. Data that is deleted or lost cannot be recovered.

  • Your Responsibility: You are highly encouraged to back up any important data to another location. ITS (Information Technology Services) is not responsible for any data loss, whether due to user error, hardware failure, or administrative error.
