Koa

Koa is the third iteration of a central high performance computing cluster at UH. Made possible by institutional investment, the UH HPC was made publically available to the UH system-wide community in 2015. Through additional investments including from the National Science Foundation (NSF) and UH Principal Investigators, the cluster grew into Mana and leveraged existing computational resources. In 2023 Koa was established through additional NSF investment and continues to leverage existing computational and storage resources.

Timeline

 

Timeline

  • 2015

    The first central HPC at UH, named the UH-HPC, was made available to all of the UH community  

  • 2018

    The second central HPC at UH, named Mana, was made available to all the UH community.  Mana incorporated many of the computational resources that the UH-HPC provided

  • 2023

    The third central HPC at UH, named Koa, was made available to all the UH community.  Koa incorporated  Mana's computational resources and some of the resources provided by the UH-HPC were finally decommissioned.
