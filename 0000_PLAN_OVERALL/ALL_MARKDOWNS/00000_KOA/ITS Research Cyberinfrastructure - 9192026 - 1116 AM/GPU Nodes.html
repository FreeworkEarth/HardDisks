GPU Nodes

As Koa is the 3rd generation central HPC cluster at UH, in which it has consumed previous cluster generation hardware, it currently has a long tail of different types of CPUs available to users.  The GPUs available to users go as far back as Circa 2018 with the Nvidia GeForce RTX 2070 GPUs  and reach into modern day with Nvidia H200 NVL GPUs.
