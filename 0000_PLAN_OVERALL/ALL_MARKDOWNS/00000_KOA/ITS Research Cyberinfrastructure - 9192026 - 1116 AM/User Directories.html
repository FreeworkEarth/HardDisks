Layout

With a new account on Koa, a user will have the following top level structure provided in their home directory

[itsrci@vn-00-00-00 ~]$ ls -la
total 9.0K
drwxr-xr-x  3 itsrci itsrci   3 Jul 22 22:54 .local
drwxr-xr-x  3 itsrci itsrci   3 Jul 22 22:54 examples
-rw-r--r--  1 itsrci itsrci 193 Jul 22 22:54 .bash_profile
-rw-r--r--  1 itsrci itsrci  18 Jul 22 22:54 .bash_logout
-rw-r--r--  1 itsrci itsrci  13 Jul 22 22:54 .vimrc
-rw-r--r--  1 itsrci itsrci 231 Jul 22 22:54 .bashrc
-rw-r--r--  1 itsrci itsrci 334 Jul 22 22:54 .emacs
-rw-r--r--  1 itsrci itsrci  14 Jul 22 22:54 .condarc
drwx------  2 itsrci itsrci   5 Jul 22 22:54 .ssh
lrwxrwxrwx  1 root     root      32 Jul 22 22:54 koa_scratch -> /mnt/lustre/koa/scratch/itsrci
drwxr-x---  7 itsrci itsrci  14 Jul 22 22:54 .
drwxr-xr-x 16 root     root       0 Jul 25 20:34 ..

The user is provided with a few pre-made configuration files and folders and also provided a directory with examples and a single symlink labeled koa_scratch which is a shortcut to their personal scratch directory.

If a user becomes part of a class, a lab or koastore storage allocation, typically the user will be provided additional symlinks to provide quick and easy shortcuts to these locations.
